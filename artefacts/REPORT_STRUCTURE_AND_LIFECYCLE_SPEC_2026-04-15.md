# Report Structure And Lifecycle Spec

## Lifecycle
1. `notes`: background capture of approved fragments and evidence
2. `plan`: section structure, key messages, evidence mapping
3. `draft`: full report from approved material
4. `refine`: tighten logic, clarity, and board-readiness
5. `final`: present polished output

## Expected Report Shape
- Executive assessment
- What the data actually shows
- Forecast
- What is broken
- Root causes
- What to fix
- Strategic direction
- Required missing data
- Risks and assumptions

## Formatting Rules
- Australian English
- AUD currency formatting: `A$X,XXX.XX`
- one decimal place for percentages
- explicit facts vs estimates vs assumptions
- concise board-ready structure with no filler
