# Ride Electric: Full Business Analysis and Strategy

**Sources:** `RIDE_ELECTRIC_MARGIN_AND_MOVEMENT_ANALYSIS_2026-04-15.md`, `RIDE_ELECTRIC_MONTHLY_REVENUE_BUCKETS_2026-04-15.md`  
**Date:** 2026-04-15  

This document ties **group-level** margin and marketing movement (all five Odoo entities: EBD, Wholesale, Retail, Brisbane, Burleigh) to **storefront-level** revenue composition (Retail, Brisbane, Burleigh only). Where the two lenses do not align, that is called out.

---

## 1. How to read this analysis

**Group metrics (margin artefact):** Revenue, COGS, GP, GP%, and marketing spend proxy include `EBD` and `Ride Electric Wholesale` as well as the three customer-facing companies. Month-on-month and flags refer to this consolidated view.

**Revenue buckets (buckets artefact):** Workshop, FatFish core, FatBoy core, and Everything Else are **only** for Retail, Brisbane, and Burleigh. They do not include EBD/Wholesale. FatBoy “core” is inferred from line descriptions where Odoo does not use a dedicated FatBoy income account; treat FatBoy series as directional, not audit-grade.

**Strategic implication:** When group revenue spikes but bucket “Everything Else” is flat, part of the move is often **EBD/Wholesale or inter-entity flows**, not only the three stores’ product mix.

---

## 2. Strategic thesis (what the combined data supports)

1. **Margin quality has improved structurally.** FY2025 vs FY2024: revenue +87%, COGS +77%, GP +139%; GP% rose from 16.2% to 20.7%. FY2026 YTD vs comparable prior-year slice: revenue +20%, COGS +5%, GP +98%; GP% rose from 16.4% to 27.0%. Revenue and COGS correlate strongly (0.94); GP tracks revenue (0.74) but **outperformance** is mix and cost recognition, not marketing alone.

2. **The operating model shifted toward Retail-led scale and FatFish-led product revenue.** FatFish core sales explode from FY2024 into FY2025 and again in FY2026 YTD. Burleigh is a major FatFish venue; Retail is the largest absolute pool.

3. **FatBoy became a material second pillar in FY2025 and FY2026 YTD** under the name-based definition. Burleigh carries a large share of FatBoy core in many months—worth validating operationally (demand vs allocation).

4. **Workshop is a deliberate recurring-revenue layer.** Workshop totals rise FY2024 → FY2025 → FY2026 YTD; Burleigh’s workshop line accelerates hardest. Strategy: protect workshop capacity, attach service to bike sales, and report workshop as a KPI line in board packs.

5. **Marketing proxy does not one-for-one explain revenue or GP.** Several months show marketing up while revenue or GP is down (flags). Interpret as timing, mix, ledger classification, or channel lag—not proof that “marketing failed” in isolation.

6. **Wholesale wind-down and Retail takeover** (from prior board narrative) align with **group** volatility in months where COGS and revenue diverge; EBD remains active—do not conflate EBD cessation with Wholesale.

## 4. Cross-cutting strategy recommendations

| Theme | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Margin** | Anchor board narrative on **GP% and GP dollars** trend (FY24 → FY25 → FY26 YTD), not revenue alone. |
| **FatFish** | Treat Nov–Dec and Oct–Feb as **inventory and cash windows**; scenario-plan stock and supplier terms. |
| **FatBoy** | Validate name-based classification with finance; consider **dedicated income accounts** for cleaner board reporting. |
| **Workshop** | Set targets: workshop revenue per store, attach rate to bike sales, repeat customer %; Burleigh is the **workshop scale proof point**. |
| **Marketing** | For months flagged **mkt_up_rev_down**, run a standard post-mortem: spend by vendor, campaign dates, and revenue recognition week. |
| **Wholesale / EBD** | When group MoM and store buckets **diverge**, pull **entity-level** revenue for that month before concluding store performance. |
| **Data** | Fix Shopify ROI helper for board; keep using **direct Odoo** for finance and bucket definitions documented in artefacts. |
