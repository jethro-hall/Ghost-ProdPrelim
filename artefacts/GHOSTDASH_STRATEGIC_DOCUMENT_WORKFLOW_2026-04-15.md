# GhostDASH Strategic Document Workflow

## Intent
Build Ghost Chat so strategic business documents are developed through controlled discussion, approved evidence, and staged drafting rather than generic AI writing.

## Roles
- `Business Strategist`: active operator in the main conversation.
- `Business Marketing & Strategy Documenter`: passive background compiler until explicitly invoked.
- `Odoo Specialist`: ERP-first retrieval role for exact governed evidence.

## Workflow
1. User starts a new workflow conversation.
2. Strategist leads the discussion and pressures for truth.
3. Strategist produces compact approval-ready outputs.
4. User approves or rejects each candidate fragment.
5. Approved fragments are stored in the shared `document_frame`.
6. Documenter uses only approved material plus grounded sources when invoked.
7. Final document claims remain traceable to approved discussion outputs, uploaded docs, web research, or Odoo evidence.

## Non-Negotiables
- source truth over eloquence
- operator control over autonomous drafting
- explicit approval over silent promotion
- grounded evidence over plausible filler
- strategic usefulness over polished but weak prose

## Shared State
- `workflow_mode` binds the conversation role
- `document_frame` is the canonical shared container for approved fragments
- approved fragments are the main bridge between strategist and documenter
