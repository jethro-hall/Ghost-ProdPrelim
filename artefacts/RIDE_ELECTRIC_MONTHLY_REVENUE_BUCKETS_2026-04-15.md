# Ride Electric Monthly Revenue Buckets

## Scope
Month-by-month revenue buckets for the three operating customer-facing businesses only:

- `Ride Electric Retail`
- `Ride Electric Brisbane`
- `Ride Electric Burleigh`

Excluded from these bucket tables:

- `EBD`
- `Ride Electric Wholesale`

## Classification Rules
- `Workshop Income`: posted income lines on `Sales - Workshop Repairs & Service`.
- `FatFish Core Sales`: posted income lines on FatFish bike sales accounts only.
- `FatFish Excluded`: FatFish parts, merchandise, and mixed `Bikes & Parts` lines are excluded from the FatFish bucket and also excluded from `Everything Else`.
- `FatBoy Core Sales`: no dedicated FatBoy income account family exists in Odoo, so FatBoy was classified from posted income line names. Core sales include model lines such as `Scrambler`, `Harlem`, `Bagus`, `DNA`, and `eBike` variants only.
- `FatBoy Excluded`: FatBoy parts and accessories such as chargers, pedals, mirrors, baskets, racks, motors, batteries, and similar items were excluded from the FatBoy bucket and also excluded from `Everything Else`.
- `Everything Else Sales`: all other posted income for Retail, Brisbane, and Burleigh after removing workshop, FatFish, and FatBoy-related excluded lines.
